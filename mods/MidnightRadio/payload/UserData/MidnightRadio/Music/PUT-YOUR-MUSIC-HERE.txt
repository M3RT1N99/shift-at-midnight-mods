Drop your own audio files into this folder.

Recommended: .ogg or .wav
Also attempted: .mp3 and .aiff/.aif (codec availability can vary by game build)

ffmpeg is used by the optional URL downloader. It does not add local-file
formats to Unity's audio decoder.

Subfolders are scanned too, so you can organise by album or mood.
Then open the radio panel in game (default key: F4) and hit "Reload".

-- CURRENT DEVELOPMENT BUILD ---------------------------------------------

Playback is local-only. Lobby synchronisation, a shared queue and direct
audio transfer are not active yet. Nothing in this folder, including content
hashes or URLs, is sent to other players.

This mod ships no music of its own.
